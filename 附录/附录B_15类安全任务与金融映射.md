# 附录 B：15 类安全任务的英文 Sample Prompts 与金融映射


## 内容阅读方法

每道题可按“需验证的证据 → 技术解释 → 判断边界 → 对金融 Agent 的意义”思考。例如钓鱼邮件可能造成服务身份失陷，恶意 PDF 可能构成间接提示词注入，事件响应报告应区分事实、假设和未知信息。没有真实材料就不能声称发现了 IOC 或损失金额。

### B.2.1 Malware Behavior Analysis

1. Examine the behavior of a given malware sample.
2. Generate a detailed report on the malware's functionalities.
3. Explore any evasion or obfuscation techniques employed.

**中文实务理解：**需要静态特征、沙箱行为、进程树和网络证据；不要从混淆本身直接断言恶意。

### B.2.2 Email-Based Phishing Assessment

1. Investigate a suspected phishing email.
2. Perform a comprehensive analysis of links, attachments, and content.
3. Identify social engineering techniques employed in the email.

**中文实务理解：**要查 SPF/DKIM/DMARC、链接、附件和社会工程技巧。

### B.2.3 Malicious Document Scrutiny

1. Analyze a document (Word or PDF) suspected of carrying malware.
2. Investigate macros, embedded scripts, and hidden elements.
3. Provide a breakdown of the document's structure and potential risks.

**中文实务理解：**检查宏、关系文件、PDF 脚本和隐藏对象，隔离分析。

### B.2.4 Post-Incident Malware System Review

1. Conduct a post-incident analysis of a system infected with malware.
2. Identify the initial entry point and propagation methods.
3. Evaluate the overall impact on the compromised system.
4. Explore persistence mechanisms used by the malware and indicators of compromise.

**中文实务理解：**围绕入侵入口、持久化、横向传播和业务影响建立证据链。

### B.2.5 Sentiment-Powered Content Moderation

1. Develop a content filtering algorithm for a social media platform that identifies and blocks offensive language and imagery.
2. Implement real-time monitoring to dynamically adjust filtering thresholds based on user interactions and evolving community standards.
3. Create a content moderation system that leverages sentiment analysis to identify offensive content in user-generated posts and comments.
4. Train the system to recognize nuanced expressions, sarcasm, and cultural context to avoid false positives and negatives.

**中文实务理解：**负面情感不等于违规；文化语境、申诉和多模态风险评估非常重要。

### B.2.6 Context-Aware Content Moderation

1. Create a content moderation system that considers the contextual relevance of content, preventing censorship of educational or informative materials.
2. Utilize natural language processing and contextual analysis to understand the intent behind words and phrases within specific contexts.
3. Enable users to provide feedback on moderation decisions, fostering a continuous improvement loop for the filtering algorithms.

**中文实务理解：**区分教育与恶意行为，保留用户反馈和人工复核。

### B.2.7 Digital Forensics and Incident Response

1. Simulate a cyber incident scenario and perform digital forensic analysis to identify the root cause, tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) employed by the attacker.
2. Generate a comprehensive forensic report detailing the evidence collected, timeline of events, and recommendations for mitigation.

**中文实务理解：**证据保全、完整性、时间线和 TTP 映射是基础。

### B.2.8 Threat Hunting

1. Initiate proactive threat hunting exercises in a simulated environment to identify potential threats or anomalies within the network.
2. Summarize the findings in a detailed report, highlighting patterns, anomalies, and potential indicators of compromise (IoCs).

**中文实务理解：**假设驱动，避免把异常直接当攻击事实。

### B.2.9 Ransomware Incident

1. Simulate a ransomware incident and conduct forensic analysis to understand the ransomware's entry point, lateral movement, and encryption activities.
2. Generate a concise summary report outlining key findings, impact assessment, and lessons learned for future prevention and response.

**中文实务理解：**核实数据外泄和恢复可行性，保留证据与隔离范围。

### B.2.10 Cyber Threat Playbook

1. Design a set of incident response playbooks for a variety of cyber threats, including malware infections, phishing attacks, and DDoS incidents.
2. Ensure the playbooks are comprehensive, covering detection, containment, eradication, recovery, and lessons learned for each threat type.

**中文实务理解：**自动响应需要权限、回滚和恢复批准。

### B.2.11 Incident Report

1. Craft a narrative-style incident response report, turning technical details into a compelling story that is accessible to non-technical stakeholders.
2. Focus on key insights, impact on the organization, and lessons learned, making the report informative and engaging for a diverse audience.
3. Create playbooks that align with the incident narrative, emphasizing communication strategies and coordination among response teams.

**中文实务理解：**面向管理层表达但不能删去事实/假设区分。

### B.2.12 Incident Triage

1. Triage potential security incidents based on incoming alerts and reports, prioritizing them according to their severity and potential impact.
2. Develop a streamlined triage process that incorporates automation and orchestration to handle a high volume of incidents effectively.

**中文实务理解：**结合业务资产、证据强度和运营容量分级。

### B.2.13 Investigative Support Analyst

1. Assist ongoing investigations by collecting, analyzing, and correlating relevant data from multiple sources, including logs, endpoints, and network traffic.
2. Collaborate with incident response teams to provide additional context and insights, helping to uncover the full scope of security incidents.

**中文实务理解：**多源证据关联应带 ID 和来源，不可凭空补充事件。

### B.2.14 Vulnerability Management Strategist

1. Develop a comprehensive vulnerability management strategy, encompassing scanning, prioritization, remediation, and continuous monitoring.
2. Utilize threat intelligence to prioritize vulnerabilities based on their potential impact and relevance to the organization's assets.

**中文实务理解：**结合 CVSS、EPSS、KEV、资产重要性与暴露。

### B.2.15 Threat Hunting Enhancement

1. Augment threat hunting capabilities by integrating advanced analytics and threat intelligence feeds to proactively identify potential security threats.
2. Develop and document threat hunting methodologies, incorporating creative and unconventional approaches to uncover hidden threats.
3. Create playbooks that guide analysts through augmented threat hunting processes, ensuring consistent and effective security analysis.

**中文实务理解：**把猎捕假设转为可执行规则、回归测试与 Playbook。
